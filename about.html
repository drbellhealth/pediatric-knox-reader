About Knoxville Pediatric Chiropractor Reader

An independent local-information site about pediatric chiropractic care in Knoxville, Tennessee, with a focus on the family practice at Bell Family Chiropractic in West Knoxville.

What This Site Is

This site exists to help Knoxville-area parents understand what pediatric chiropractic actually involves — what the techniques look like, what the evidence supports, what a first visit is like, and who the right candidate is — before they pick up the phone. The information here is general and educational; it isn't a substitute for an in-person evaluation or pediatric medical advice. For booking, screening, insurance verification, or anything specific to a child's case, contact the practice directly.

About the Practice We Cover

Bell Family Chiropractic offers pediatric chiropractic care alongside its broader family practice in West Knoxville. The pediatric side of the practice handles infants, toddlers, school-age kids, and teens using gentle, age-appropriate technique, with parent education and pediatrician coordination as part of the standard workflow. The office is located at 111 Sherlake Lane, Suite 101, Knoxville, TN 37922, and the main number is +1 865-383-7730.

How We Maintain Independence

This site is editorial — not the official website of the practice. We summarize publicly available information in plain language and link to the practice for booking and contact. We don't accept advertising and we don't take payment from the practice for coverage. For booking, billing, screening, or any clinical question, contact the office directly.
